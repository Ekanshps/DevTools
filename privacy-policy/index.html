DevArea Tools / Privacy policy

A straightforward note
about privacy.

The tools are built to keep inputs in the browser. This policy explains what that does—and does not—mean.

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Information you enter into the tools

The JWT inspector and CSS clamp generator are designed to process their inputs in your browser. The source code does not send JWT tokens or calculator inputs to a decoding or calculation endpoint. Do not assume that local processing makes a shared device or browser extension safe; protect credentials and close the page when you finish.

Contact messages

This static site uses an email link for contact rather than a built-in contact form. If you choose to email the maintainer, the information in that message is handled by your email provider and the recipient's email service.

Hosting and server logs

The hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, user-agent, requested path and timestamps as part of normal delivery and security logging. The exact retention and processing details depend on the production hosting provider and its settings; review the provider's current privacy terms.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The source bundle does not include an analytics SDK, advertising pixel, account system or intentional cookie-based tracking. If these are added later, this policy should be updated before publication. The hosting platform or linked external sites may have their own policies.

External links

Links to documentation, DevArea and the personal developer blog lead to other websites. Their content and data handling are governed by those sites, not this project.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or hosting practices change. The date at the top should be refreshed when a material change is published.
