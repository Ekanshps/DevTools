Cookie use in this release

The published source does not intentionally set analytics, advertising or preference cookies. The utilities work without an account and do not require a cookie to preserve JWT or CSS calculator inputs between page loads.

Hosting and third-party services

The hosting provider or external websites you visit from links may set or process cookies under their own practices. Those behaviors are controlled by the provider and its current deployment settings, not by this static website source.

If tracking is introduced later

Any future analytics, advertising or preference storage should be reviewed before being added. This page should be updated to describe the actual technologies and controls in the deployed site.
